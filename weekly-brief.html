Edition № 4 · w/e 12 October 2026 · Compiled from 7 modules, 4 regions · Professional tier

What actually moved in global securities services this week — and the one conversation each move should trigger. Every data point carries its label.

01Fund administration had its biggest consolidation week of the year

On 6 October UBS agreed to transfer its Swiss and Luxembourg fund administration businesses — including the legacy Credit Suisse traditional and alternatives books — to Northern Trust, exiting fund administration altogether; the deal is expected to close in 2Q 2027 and migrate in phases over two years. The same day CACEIS agreed to acquire Edmond de Rothschild’s third-party asset servicing business in Luxembourg (subject to CSSF approval), and KKR agreed to buy private-capital administrator Gen II Fund Services for a $5.1bn enterprise value. FACT

Why it matters: scale is concentrating into a handful of platforms, and every divestment releases a book of clients that must be re-homed. UBS exiting fund admin, CACEIS absorbing Edmond de Rothschild’s Luxembourg book and KKR buying Gen II are three separate pools of displaced clients and migrating funds in the same week.

The servicing read: lead with operational continuity at scale, not custody. Open with a transition-readiness assessment for any manager whose provider just changed hands — data migration, NAV continuity, depositary and TA re-papering — and position a front-to-back operating model that spans traditional and tokenised assets. FACT ASSESSMENT

02The week's other movers

Europe
BNY expanded its Digital Asset Custody platform into the EU under MiCA (8 Oct), after its SA/NV entity joined the ESMA MiCA register in July 2026 — one of the first G-SIBs offering regulated digital-asset custody in the region, covering BTC, ETH, SOL and USDC. FACT
APAC
Standard Chartered unveiled plans for institutional digital-asset custody in Singapore (8 Oct) — selected crypto assets, stablecoins and tokenised RWAs, sitting inside its Financing & Securities Services business and extending its UAE, Luxembourg and Hong Kong footprint. FACT
Europe · Reg
ESMA told licensed CASPs to stop servicing non-MiCA-compliant stablecoins (8 Oct), requiring existing exposures to be remediated within three months; the interim MiCA register now lists 370 CASP entries across 27 EU/EEA states. FACT
India
AMFI August data: industry AUM ₹87.07 lakh crore (+1.5% MoM), and the SIF category reached ₹31,175 crore across 33 funds and 17 AMCs, up from ₹4,928 crore in January; Q2 FY27 average AUM ₹87.22 lakh crore, +13.1% YoY. FACT
New Rails
Tokenised cash went institutional: Crédit Agricole Assurances invested €100m in a tokenised unit of Amundi’s Cash EUR money-market fund, with CACEIS on tokenisation and custody (8 Oct); BlackRock pitched tokenised MMFs as direct on-chain collateral (BUIDL ~$2.5bn); Franklin Templeton launched Hong Kong’s first tokenised USD money-market fund. FACT
Middle East
Saudi CMA capped public money-market funds’ overseas investment at 5% of NAV (circular of 8 Sep), with a six-month accelerated timeline above 20% and an investment-grade counterparty requirement; ADGM’s new fund and fund-manager rules (Institutional and Sub-Threshold Fund Manager categories) took effect 16 Sep. FACT

03What we're watching next

  • SEC custody proposal — comment period to 7 December. The 1 Oct text is open for input; track the final “qualified custodian” definition and the transition clocks that start on adoption.
  • Fund-admin migrations. UBS/Credit Suisse and Edmond de Rothschild client books change hands; watch who is named custodian, depositary and TA for the migrating funds.
  • AMFI September data — due early October. Does SIF hold past ₹31,175 crore, and does the record SIP pace sustain?
  • Digital-asset custody land-grab. BNY in the EU and Standard Chartered in Singapore put regulated digital custody on the map — watch who wins the first named MiCA and MAS custody mandates.
