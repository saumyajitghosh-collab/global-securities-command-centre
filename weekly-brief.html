What actually moved in global securities services this week — and the one conversation each move should trigger. Every data point carries its label.

01India decided to stop asking foreigners to prove who they are

On 20 November SEBI published a consultation proposing to remove the Additional Disclosure Framework for foreign portfolio investors outright — the full look-through to every natural person, with no ownership threshold, that applied to FPIs breaching concentration or size tests, and which extended to offshore-derivative subscribers and segregated portfolios. SEBI’s own case is that the framework is unique to India and goes beyond anti-money-laundering beneficial-ownership rules, which it puts at 10–15% against roughly 25% in the US, UK, Singapore, Japan and Hong Kong. Comments close 11 December 2026. The same day, chairman Tuhin Kanta Pandey said FPI onboarding could come down to five days. FACT

Why it matters: India has spent a decade as the market where a global manager’s onboarding file went to die. Remove the disclosure gate and the queue moves — but so does the reason a client tolerated a slow, bespoke operating model. The competitive question stops being “can you get me in” and becomes “what do you do once I’m in”: beneficial-ownership monitoring, scheme-level cash forecasting, and one reconciliation spine across FPI, domestic mutual fund and AIF books.

The servicing read: pair it with the settlement clocks, because they are the only part of this with a bookable number. FPI cash-market net settlement of funds is due 31 December 2026; mutual-fund scheme-level netting starts 1 April 2027 and forbids any cross-scheme funding. Custodians run both in parallel for a quarter, and the funding benefit is a hard rupee figure in a business case. FACT ASSESSMENT

02The week’s other movers

APAC · Digital
A G-SIB became issuer, tokeniser, custodian and distributor on one fund. From 19 November Hong Kong customers can open a RedCoin wallet in the HSBC HK app and buy a tokenised unit class (Class SD) of HSBC Global Money Funds – HKD from HKD 100. HSBC Securities Services tokenises the class; HSBC Digital Assets Custody safekeeps the units and the stablecoin. FACT
APAC · Rails
Tokenised deposits went multi-currency and cross-border. Standard Chartered launched them in Singapore on 19 November, after Hong Kong on 2 November — near real-time flow in SGD, HKD, USD, EUR, CNH and GBP, with China AMC, Finloop and PantherTrade live. This is the cash leg tokenised funds have been missing. FACT
APAC · Custody
Hong Kong settled the crypto-ETF structure: two tiers. HashKey, through HashKey Custody Services, became sub-custodian for the GaoTeng Bitcoin ETF listing on 20 November, with BOCI-Prudential Trustee as primary custodian holding overall responsibility. HashKey now sub-custodies seven virtual-asset ETFs. The bank trustee keeps the wrapper; the licensed exchange keeps the keys. FACT
Europe · Mandate
A named depositary loss for State Street. From 2 November Allianz Global Investors’ German UCITS range — Adiverba, Euro Rentenfonds, Thesaurus, Fondak, the Fondsvorsorge series — moved from State Street Bank International to BNY Mellon SA/NV, Frankfurt, BaFin-approved on 30 July, at no cost to unitholders. FACT
Europe · Product
Withholding tax became a custody product. BNP Paribas extended RAQUEST on 10 November to cover reporting under Germany’s new MiKaDiv procedure from January 2027. Separately CACEIS has traded under the Crédit Agricole name since 1 October. FACT
North America
Thirteen days left on the SEC custody file. Comments on S7-2026-35 close 7 December: the crypto permitted-custodian definition, conditional self-custody conditions and the new Form ADV / N-CEN tokenised-fund questions are all still open. Meanwhile CIBC Mellon launched Canadian digital asset custody on 18 November with BNY as sub-custodian — the JV template for domestic custodians that cannot build it alone. FACT
Middle East
State Street formalised an expansion with Emirates NBD. An MOU signed in Dubai on 17 November covers Emirates NBD Asset Management’s operating-model evolution, investment solutions, digital innovation and wealth, on top of a decade of fund administration and custody. ADGM separately opened Consultation Paper No. 8 of 2026 on 13 November to fold virtual assets into the FSMR framework. FACT
Mandates
Clearstream added post-trade services for FNZ Bank on top of Vestima (11 Nov); Northern Trust took Hargreaves Lansdown’s money market fund administration (6 Nov); Clear Street closed its Boom Securities (HK) acquisition on 19 November with SFC approval, taking a US prime broker into APAC custody. FACT

Assessment
The week’s real signal is structural, not numerical: banks are choosing to orchestrate tokenised rails (BNY–SWIAT, CIBC Mellon–BNY, HSBC in-app) rather than tokenise the asset. Expect 2027 RFP language to ask for orchestration evidence, not token issuance. ASSESSMENT

04What we’re watching next

  • SEBI comments — 11 December. Whether the ADF is deleted or merely softened for ODI subscribers, and whether the five-day onboarding target arrives with a named service standard attached to intermediaries.
  • SEC custody comments — 7 December. The permitted-custodian definition for crypto and the transition clocks that start on adoption. Thirteen days; custody committees are re-reading lists now.
  • Saudi CMA — 20 December. Whether the GCC-resident individual category survives consultation, and whether the retail fund rules and the 5%-holder governance overhaul land together.
  • AMFI November data — early December. Does SIF hold above ₹42,670 crore for a second month, and does the discontinuation ratio stay above 100%? That second number matters more for TA revenue than the AUM headline.
  • UAE onshore regularisation — 31 December 2026. Firms that have not re-papered under the new Capital Market Authority face fines up to AED 200m or a multiple of profit; the extension question is unresolved.
  • Two settlement go-lives — 31 December 2026 and 1 April 2027. FPI net funding first, then MF scheme-level netting. Watch which custodians publish a funding-benefit number and which stay silent.
