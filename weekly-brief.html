What actually moved in global securities services this week — and the one conversation each move should trigger. Every data point carries its label.

01Abakkus just made SIF a servicing segment, not a product

Abakkus Asset Manager announced on 22 September that its mutual-fund business received SEBI approval (28 August) to establish a Specialised Investment Fund platform branded Fokkus, with strategies across equity, debt and hybrid to follow after filings. The same day, it filed its DRHP — group managed assets above ₹40,000 crore, the mutual-fund book past ₹10,000 crore, KFin Technologies as registrar. FACT

Why this matters more than another fund launch: Abakkus already runs AIF, PMS and mutual funds. Adding SIF makes it the first manager to commit publicly to the full four-wrapper stack — MF → SIF → PMS → AIF — around one sophisticated-investor franchise. The servicing question that creates is architectural, not incremental: either four operating stacks, or one cross-wrapper architecture covering custody, NAV, derivatives servicing, collateral, IBOR, reconciliation, regulatory reporting and RTA integration.

The servicing read: SIF assets hit ₹31,175 crore in August — more than double May's ₹13,814 crore, +35% month-on-month, folios past 125,000. The thesis has upgraded: this is now a new servicing segment sitting between traditional mutual funds and alternatives, warranting a dedicated proposition rather than "another mutual-fund scheme" treatment. Flag Abakkus/Fokkus for account development — and start with fund accounting and custody, not TA: the KFin registrar relationship is already wired. FACT ASSESSMENT

02The week's other movers

India
MF AUM ₹87.08 lakh crore at end-August — over ₹1.3 lakh crore added in one month, on record SIP flows of ₹32,297 crore and 10 crore+ contributing accounts. GIFT City commitments reached $45 billion; the IFSC investor base grew 68% in the June quarter. And Motilal Oswal received SEBI's nod to launch custodian services (23 Sep) — the first broker-led domestic entrant in years, and a greenfield platform buyer. FACT
North America
US ETF AUM hit a record $16.36 trillion at end-August, with $1.41 trillion of YTD net inflows — 76.5% above the same period of 2025 — the 52nd consecutive month of inflows. Global ETF assets crossed $24 trillion for the first time. FACT
Europe
HSBC won a £21bn RLAM Irish mandate off State Street (17 Sep) — custody, depositary and fund admin across two umbrellas. Citi took a $380bn full middle-office mandate from Aegon AM (13 Aug), deepening its Aladdin-provider model. The churn now cuts both ways. FACT
Middle East
ADGM finalised its enhanced funds framework (16 Sep) — lighter regimes for sub-threshold and institutional fund managers, transition into 2027. Saudi Arabia's CMA capped overseas investments by public MMFs at 5% of NAV, re-shoring a $20.5 billion pool. FACT
Global rails
Tokenised RWA ex-stablecoins holding at ~$39.2 billion; tokenised US Treasuries at $15.9 billion. Kinexys notional past $1.5 trillion. No step-change this week — the rails keep compounding quietly. FACT

03What we're watching next

  • SEC custody amendments — October 2026 target. The crypto-specific rulemaking is at OIRA review (since 25 Aug). When the proposal text lands, every qualified-custodian conversation reopens at once — have the safeguarding proposition pre-positioned.
  • AMFI September data — early October. Does the SIF category keep its pace past ₹31,175 crore, and does the SIF folio count approach 150,000? Both would strengthen the dedicated-proposition case.
  • Abakkus Fokkus strategy disclosures. The derivative intensity of the first strategies determines whether the servicing requirement is heavy (long-short, hybrid) or moderate. Don't pre-sell the derivatives stack until the filings show it.
  • AIFMD II re-papering in Europe and the HSBC mandate-churn window — two live switch-pitch opportunities running on regulatory clocks.
