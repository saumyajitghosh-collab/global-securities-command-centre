What actually moved in global securities services this week — and the one conversation each move should trigger. Every data point carries its label.

01Tokenised collateral stopped being a pilot

Three threads closed in the same fortnight. Eligible OKX institutional clients can now post BlackRock’s BUIDL as collateral while they trade, under a framework Standard Chartered announced in April — the posted tokens stay in Standard Chartered custody and keep earning dollar yield, while BNY Mellon continues to custody the fund’s underlying assets. On 12 November Securitize and Ethena activated a $100m, 24/7 liquidity facility for BUIDL, launching with $50m ($25m USDC / $25m BUIDL) and able to top up $50m within the hour, so eligible investors can move between the fund and stablecoins outside market hours. On 4 November HSBC, Kinexys by J.P. Morgan, MUFG, Societe Generale FORGE and Standard Chartered launched the Global Layer One Organisation, taking MAS’s shared-ledger project industry-side — as a standards body, because the common ledger was never built. FACT

Why it matters: one instrument now has two custody layers, two reconciliation breaks and no publicly disclosed haircut policy. That is a new diligence, segregation and margin-mobility requirement that most institutional mandates do not yet document — and it arrives precisely as the SEC proposes to make tokenised-share custody a public disclosure item.

The servicing read: four questions to put in front of any client posting tokenised fund units for margin — which custodian holds the token, what the haircut policy is, how the two registers reconcile, and who can move the asset during a margin call. Lead with the BUIDL/Standard Chartered structure as the reference case. FACT ASSESSMENT

02The week’s other movers

India
October was stabilisation, not a rebound — and SIFs cleared ₹42,000 crore. AMFI net AUM ₹83.39 lakh crore at 31 October (+0.9% MoM), AAUM ₹85.18 lakh crore, as net inflows swung to ₹1.74 lakh crore from a ₹1.33 lakh crore outflow — ₹1.13 lakh crore of it back into liquid and overnight funds. Equity inflows moderated 13.9% to ₹32,994 crore, still the 68th positive month; SIP contribution ₹32,160 crore, SIP assets ₹17.68 lakh crore (21.2% of AUM), folios 28.78 crore. SIF assets reached ₹42,669.52 crore on ₹5,150.41 crore of inflows and three new strategies raising ₹679 crore — roughly 21 times October 2025. FACT
India · Reg
SEBI took settlement risk out of the MF cash leg. A circular of 5 November sets a framework for net settlement of funds for transactions undertaken by mutual fund schemes in the cash market — a funding, reconciliation and intraday-liquidity change that lands on custodians and fund accountants, not only on members. FACT
Middle East
Riyadh is compounding the post-QFI opening. The CMA is consulting until 20 December on account procedures: a new category for foreign individuals resident in GCC states to buy Main Market shares directly, direct access for endowments and non-profits, retained access for former Saudi and GCC residents, and simplified account opening. A parallel consultation covers retail fund rules, and a governance overhaul giving 5%-voting holders more board leverage has been out since 29 October. FACT
Middle East · UAE
The UAE is licensing faster and the fund pool is deepening. 68 new companies licensed in 9M 2026 with 30+ under review and a seven-working-day decision target; domestic funds up 94% YoY to 97 with AUM up 30% to AED 28.6bn. ADGM grew AUM 54% YoY in H1 2026, managers from 154 to 190 and funds from 209 to 276; its new Institutional and Sub-Threshold Fund Manager categories bite on 31 March 2027. FACT
North America
The AI claims became auditable numbers. BNY put more than 400 AI solutions in production in October (up from 300+) with ~150 digital employees; AI handled 10%+ of custody settlement status inquiries in Q1 2026, investigated 80%+ faster. Citi published Custody+ metrics: Single Event Processing touches 80%+ of events once, voluntary corporate actions up to 92% faster in the US with 96% of events under two hours, and AI tax servicing cutting document processing up to 70% across 100,000+ withholding-tax reclaims a year. BNY also took a second digital-asset custody mandate — the Invesco Galaxy Ethereum ETF, five-year term from 6 November, alongside Coinbase. FACT
Europe
AIFMD II transposition is a patchwork, not a date. Portugal, Greece, Cyprus, Latvia and Estonia have notified nothing; France’s ordinance has stalled to a late-February 2027 Assembly vote with the AMF on a transitional regime; Croatia is the only state reported to have gold-plated. The real clock is ESMA’s harmonised Annex IV standards from 16 April 2027 — new fields for delegation, staff resources, LMT use and loan origination, with UCITS ManCos in scope for the first time. Separately, ESMA’s 8 October opinion gives regulators three months to force remediation of non-MiCA-compliant stablecoin exposures, biting around early January 2027. FACT
APAC · Digital
Project Guardian’s ledger became a rulebook. The Global Layer One Organisation launched 4 November under HSBC, Kinexys, MUFG, Societe Generale FORGE and Standard Chartered; MAS now appears only as a listed policymaker. The output is standards, toolkits and programmable-compliance architecture — not shared infrastructure. FACT
Mandates
Clearstream won further post-trade services from FNZ Bank on top of Vestima (11 Nov); Northern Trust was appointed administrator for Hargreaves Lansdown’s money market fund (6 Nov) and asset servicing provider to Australia’s LeavePlus scheme (27 Oct); QNB Group used The Network Forum Middle East to press its State Street custody servicing model for Qatar (4 Nov). FACT

04What we’re watching next

  • SEC custody comments — 7 December. File S7-2026-35. Watch the final permitted-custodian definition and the transition clocks that start on adoption; every US manager’s custody committee re-reads its list before this date.
  • Saudi CMA — 20 December. Whether the GCC-resident individual category survives consultation intact, and whether the retail fund rules and the 5%-holder governance overhaul land together.
  • AMFI November data — early December. Does SIF hold above ₹42,670 crore through a second month, and does the first debt-oriented strategy file?
  • ADGM’s 31 March 2027 deadline. Foreign fund managers must appoint an FSRA-licensed administrator and a UAE-resident director and unwind delegation arrangements — a dated fund-admin and TA trigger.
  • ESMA Annex IV consultation. The technical standards are still unpublished but the reporting regime is expected to apply from 16 April 2027; delegation and staff-resource fields are the data-model problem.
  • DTCC tokenisation go-live. 25 securities, 24/7 wallet-to-wallet transfers — the volume test for tokenised collateral, and the natural comparator to the BUIDL structure.
