01The SEC just reopened the qualified-custodian question

On 1 October the Commission issued a sweeping custody-rule proposal (Release Nos. IA-7023, IC-36353) to modernise adviser and fund custody: it would permit adviser self-custody of crypto where no qualified custodian is available (with guardrails), add state-chartered trust companies as permitted crypto custodians, update broker-dealer custodian use by funds, and carve authorised discretionary trading and standing letters of authorisation out of the custody rule. FACT

Why it matters more than the withdrawn Safeguarding Rule: this version is narrower but operational. It turns on the definition of “qualified custodian” and on asset-segregation mechanics — the exact ground on which every custody pitch is won or lost — and it legitimises bank trust-company crypto custody, a direct opening for incumbents.

The servicing read: the trigger is now FIRED, not armed. Every qualified-custodian conversation reopens at once, so lead with safeguarding infrastructure — segregation, reconciliation, governance and reporting — rather than chain novelty. Use BNY's digital TA and Citi's digital-asset custody go-live as proof that the operating model is being decided now. FACT ASSESSMENT

02The week's other movers

New Rails
Tokenisation shifted from single assets to whole strategies — BlackRock and Ondo put three model portfolios onchain as single tokens (3–4 Oct); WisdomTree consolidated its tokenised funds under "WisdomTree Onchain" at ~$1.2bn (29 Sep); Centrifuge launched three tokenised Treasury/credit funds on Arc (4 Oct). Brazil's CSD went live with Ripple on the XRP Ledger, mirroring BTG Pactual fund shares (29 Sep) — a licensed depository running live records on a public chain. FACT
North America
State Street re-entered US RIA custody with "State Street Wealth Services" (3–4 Oct), targeting RIAs with $1bn+ via an Apex Fintech partnership. Custodian scale at 30 Jun 2026: BNY $62.6tn AUC/A ($8.6tn serviced), State Street $57.9tn, Citi ~$35tn. FACT
APAC
State Street was named Wellington Management's transfer agent on HKEX's Integrated Fund Platform (29 Sep) — executing Wellington's first IFP subscription/redemption and remaining the platform's only global TA: a distribution land-grab on the rail into China. FACT
India
SIF concentration is now stark: the top-3 platforms — Altiva ₹10,785 cr, iSIF ₹5,430 cr, Magnum ₹5,119 cr — hold 68.5% of the ₹31,175 cr category (Aug), which is up 125.7% YoY across 33 schemes and 17 AMCs. FACT
Europe
The FCA extended its FRAME fund-reporting consultation to 22 Oct 2026 (from 22 Sep), and ESMA's new Union Strategic Supervisory Priority on digital innovation (AI and tokenisation) starts in 2027, pulling CASPs further into DORA custody supervision. FACT

03What we're watching next

  • SEC custody proposal — comment period ahead. The 1 Oct text is now open for input. Track the final qualified-custodian definition; the 12-month (≥$1bn RAUM) and 18-month transition clocks start on adoption.
  • AMFI September data — due early October. Does SIF hold past ₹31,175 crore and push folios toward 150,000, and does the record SIP pace sustain?
  • Tokenised-strategy servicing model. BlackRock–Ondo and CSD BR–Ripple put onchain and offchain books in the same conversation — watch who is named custodian and TA for the next wave.
  • HKEX IFP and State Street's wealth re-entry — two live land-grabs (Asia fund distribution, US RIA custody) that reshape the competitive map.
