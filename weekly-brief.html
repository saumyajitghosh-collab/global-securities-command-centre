01The SEC rewrote the custody rule — and wrote crypto into it

On 1 October the SEC proposed new Investment Company Act custody rules and amendments to the Advisers Act rule, redesignated Rule 223-1, published in the Federal Register on 6 October. The package adds a crypto custody framework — state trust companies become permitted custodians and advisers get a narrow, heavily conditioned self-custody route — and modernises the traditional rule: regulated funds may use any broker-dealer custodian subject to Exchange Act Rule 15c3-3, physical segregation and board ratification of custodial contracts go, the PCAOB registration requirement for accountants is dropped, and Forms ADV and N-CEN gain disclosure on crypto custody and tokenised fund shares. Comments close 7 December 2026 (File S7-2026-35). FACT

Why it matters: the permitted-custodian definition is the single biggest determinant of mandate flow, and this proposal reopens it — both by widening who qualifies and by making tokenised-share custody a public disclosure item. Custodian selection becomes a board-visible, examinable decision rather than a procurement detail.

The servicing read: lead with permitted-custodian and N-CEN readiness, not custody pricing. The first conversations should be with funds whose custodian is a broker-dealer relying on 15c3-3 and with any manager holding tokenised share classes — both face a disclosure change regardless of how the final rule lands. FACT ASSESSMENT

02The week’s other movers

North America
Q3 confirmed the servicing upcycle — and exposed the backlog behind it. BNY reported AUC/A of $63.4trn (+10% YoY) and Securities Services revenue of $2.82bn (+15%), its 15th consecutive quarter of year-on-year sales growth. State Street reported a record $59.2trn AUC/A (+15%), $429bn of new servicing asset wins, $68m of annualised fee wins and $332m of annualised servicing fees still awaiting installation. Citi’s mandate win rate is up 40% YoY; Northern Trust posted 8% fee growth. FACT
India
SIF crossed ₹38,000 crore in its first year. Net assets reached ₹38,286.94 crore at 30 September across 34 schemes, 17 AMCs and 1,56,483 folios, from ₹2,010 crore in October 2025 — with ₹7,578 crore of September inflows taken in a month the Nifty 50 TRI fell 6.0%, and no debt strategy yet launched. FACT
India · Reg
SEBI opened two distribution doors. The board of 24 September approved PRIM — portfolio managers investing in direct MF plans, ETFs and SIFs at a ₹25 lakh minimum with a 1% fee cap — and manager-led accredited-investor accreditation, notified for AIFs on 15 October with a securities-market-exposure test of ₹5 crore for individuals and ₹20 crore for bodies corporate, lifting the eligible pool from ~96,000 to ~3,70,000 investors. FACT
APAC
Hong Kong set a T+1 date. HKEX’s consultation conclusions of 16 October target T+1 in the second half of 2028 on a three-layer model: post-trade infrastructure changes, workflow tools including a Settlement Profile Repository and SSI status dashboard, and industry coordination. Southbound Stock Connect advances with Hong Kong. FACT
APAC · Digital
Standard Chartered consolidated digital custody. Definitive agreements signed 21 October to acquire 100% of Zodia Custody, folding regulated entities in Singapore, Hong Kong, Australia, the UAE and Luxembourg into Securities Services, with the software arm spun into Zodia Solutions. SC Bank (Singapore) plans institutional custody of selected cryptoassets, stablecoins and tokenised RWAs by year-end. FACT
Europe
Two clocks are now running to April 2027. AIFMD II liquidity-management-tool rules apply, with a transitional period for existing AIFs and UCITS to 16 April 2027 and ESMA’s expanded Annex IV reporting expected from that April. Separately the EBA designated USDC the EU’s first significant e-money token on 14 October, taking prudential oversight from the ACPR on 12 November 2026. FACT
Middle East
Riyadh is reviewing the 49% foreign-ownership cap. Confirmed 5 October; no new percentage announced, and the CMA is consulting sector regulators. The QFI regime and swap framework were already abolished from 1 February 2026, leaving the 49% aggregate and 10% single non-resident limits binding. The UAE CMA confirmed a December 2026 AI and digital-transformation launch and further free-zone/mainland rules within two months. FACT

04What we’re watching next

  • SEC custody comments — 7 December. Watch the final permitted-custodian definition and the transition clocks that start on adoption.
  • AMFI October data. Does SIF hold above ₹38,287 crore, and does the first debt-oriented strategy file?
  • DTCC tokenisation go-live. 25 securities, 24/7 wallet-to-wallet transfers and the first named participants — the volume test for tokenised collateral.
  • Hong Kong T+1 readiness. SSI data quality and settlement-instruction automation are where the operating-model spend lands first.
  • UAE CMA in December. The AI and digital-transformation announcement plus free-zone/mainland rules, ahead of the 1 January 2027 regularisation deadline.
  • UBS/Credit Suisse fund-admin migration. Closing expected 2Q 2027, phased over two years — watch who is named depositary and TA for the migrating books.
