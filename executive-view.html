Coverage follows evidence: start where triggers have fired and whitespace is widest, expand into the regulatory windows, scale into the big pools once references exist. ASSESSMENT — sequencing judgement, anchored to FACT triggers above.

  • Launch the SIF derivatives-servicing proposition — the ₹31,175 cr pool doubled in three months; AMC SIF franchises need collateral, margin, derivatives lifecycle and exposure monitoring from day one.
  • Pilot the India Alternatives Stack with 3–5 AIF GPs: admin + TA + custody bundled (₹16.94 lakh cr commitments growing ~25–30%/yr).
  • Position the GIFT City launch kit for global AMCs eyeing the IFSC route.

Months 3–6

02Expand — Middle East + Europe windows

  • KSA capacity pitch: Saudi fund assets racing past $250B toward $350B while 100% of custodians report capacity gaps — the under-served market of the five.
  • ADGM digital-custody entry: +215% AUM growth and the region's clearest digital-asset regulatory perimeter.
  • Europe re-papering window: AIFMD II liquidity-portfolio-data and DORA-resilience offers; chase the HSBC-exit mandate churn; target the 332 relocated firms' servicing needs.
  • Open SWF-adjacent conversations (PIF $925B, ADIA, Mubadala) on cross-border servicing benchmarks.
Exit criteria: 5 mandates across ≥2 new regions · 1 sovereign-adjacent engagement · Europe pipeline anchored to a dated regulatory deadline
Months 7–12

03Scale — North America + global rails

  • ETF servicing push: $15.7T US ETF market, $1.12T record 2024 inflows, active ETFs the fastest-growing segment ($313B) — TA and fund-accounting volume follows flows.

  • Digital-rails productisation: BUIDL-style tokenised fund servicing (custody + transfer + NAV on-chain); Kinexys $1.5T notional proves institutional demand.
  • Agentic-AI operating pitch: BNY Eliza at 125+ production solutions (+175% adoption) resets the cost curve — sell the operating-model transformation, not the tool.
Exit criteria: Cross-region multi-shore mandates in play · digital-rail proposition priced and piloted · measurable AUA sourced
05 · The Narrative

The Convergence Thesis

The one-slide story behind every module — and the reason a provider with custody + fund services + technology can win now.

Custody, fund administration and technology are converging into one mandate

BNY runs a $31.2T TA platform alongside its custody book; State Street attributes $380B of its Q2-2025 AUC/A wins to Alpha, its integrated platform; SS&C and Broadridge bundle software with services; Citi's Custody+ and JPMorgan's Kinexys ($1.5T notional) pull settlement rails and asset servicing together. The league table says scale wins at the top — $180T across four names — but the growth in servicing revenue is being created where the products are getting more complex: derivatives-enabled SIFs in India, private capital in North America, tokenised funds on new rails, and SWF mandates in the Gulf. Complexity, not size, is what an ambitious provider can actually sell against. ASSESSMENT

The strategic implication: the winning proposition in 2026–27 is custody + derivatives servicing + collateral/margin + NAV/fund accounting + exposure controls + investor servicing + regulatory reporting, delivered as one stack, priced for the bundle — because that is the shape of the products regulators are now licensing. ASSESSMENT

06 · Anticipate The Room

Board-level questions, pre-answered

The five questions a CEO, COO or board member will ask about this coverage strategy — with the evidence-backed answer.

Where is the nearest $50M-servicing-revenue pool?
Not the headline AUC/A numbers — those are contested by four $40T+ incumbents. The near pools are India alternatives (₹16.94 lakh cr AIF commitments +25.6% YoY, with SIF assets doubling to ₹31,175 cr in three months), US private capital admin ($7.7T and the fastest-growing segment), and Saudi fund administration ($250B→$350B where 100% of custodians report capacity gaps). Each is growing faster than the incumbent infrastructure around it. FACT on the sizes; ASSESSMENT on revenue conversion.
What is the fastest path to a reference win?
India, months 1–2: the SIF trigger has just fired and the proposition — derivatives + collateral + fund accounting for SIF franchises — has no incumbent answer because the category is three months old at scale. A single AMC SIF mandate is a bankable reference for the entire derivatives-servicing stack. ASSESSMENT anchored to FACT (AMFI Aug 2026 data)
Which single regulatory date matters most?
DORA enforcement in Europe (live since Jan 2025, escalating) and AIFMD II delegated-administration requirements (Apr 2026): both force every fund-services buyer in Europe to review provider arrangements — a mandatory re-papering conversation. In North America, the pending SEC custody/safeguarding rule is the armed trigger: when it lands, every qualified-custodian conversation reopens. FACT
Why would we win against BNY, State Street and JPMorgan?
Not on their field — on adjacency. They are optimised for scale in plain-vanilla pools (the $180T top-4 concentration proves it). The growth pools are derivative-heavy, tokenisation-adjacent and regulator-new: SIFs, private capital, ADGM digital custody, GIFT City. In those, the incumbent's advantage is smaller and the buyer's problem is freshness of expertise. Sell where the product was invented after the incumbent's platform. ASSESSMENT
What does the digital-rails investment actually buy?
Option value with a visible deadline. Tokenised RWA at $39.2B (Sep 2026) is small against $180T custody — but Kinexys at $1.5T notional, BUIDL at $2.8B and Progmat's ¥452B show institutional rails forming. The buy is a beachhead in fund-on-chain servicing (custody, transfer, NAV) that becomes the default when regulators license tokenised funds at scale — as Germany, Luxembourg and the ADGM already have. FACT on figures; ASSESSMENT on timing
What is the single biggest risk to this plan?
India concentration in months 1–2: if the SIF/AIF beachhead stalls on regulatory caution, the sequence slips. Mitigation: the Europe re-papering window runs on its own regulatory clock and does not depend on winning India — it can be pulled forward. The model above is deliberately re-weightable for exactly this. ASSESSMENT
