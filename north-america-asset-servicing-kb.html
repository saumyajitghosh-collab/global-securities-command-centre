04 · Regulatory Landscape

A custody rule still pending, T+1 live, and a crypto framework now open for banks.

The SEC Safeguarding Rule remains proposed, not finalised — the single biggest source of structural uncertainty. T+1 has been live since May 2024. And the 2025 regulatory shift (SAB 121 rescinded, OCC IL 1184, GENIUS Act) has opened the door for bank digital-asset custody. Each date below is a sales trigger.

SEC Safeguarding Rule PROPOSED

Proposed Feb 15, 2023 (Rule 223-1) to replace the custody rule (Rule 206(4)-2) under the Investment Advisers Act. Expands scope from “funds and securities” to all client assets (crypto, real estate, derivatives). Requires written adviser-custodian agreements, defines “possession or control,” comment period reopened to Oct 30, 2023. Status as of Sep 2026: NOT YET FINALISED. If adopted: ≥$1B RAUM advisers get 12 months; others 18 months. FACT

DTCC T+1 Settlement LIVE

May 28, 2024 — US transitioned from T+2 to T+1 for most broker-dealer transactions in cash equities, corporate debt and UITs. Canada transitioned May 27, 2024. New Rule 15c6-2 requires allocations, confirmations and affirmations (ACA) by end of trade date. Impact: compressed timelines for corporate actions, FX, reconciliation; cover/protect period shortened from 2 to 1 business day. FACT

OCC Digital-Asset Custody DLT

IL 1170 (Jul 2020): national banks may provide crypto custody. IL 1183 (2025) reaffirmed. IL 1184 (May 7, 2025): banks may buy/sell assets held in custody at customer direction and outsource crypto activities subject to risk management. Joint OCC/Fed/FDIC statement (2025): “Crypto-Asset Safekeeping by Banking Organizations.” FACT

SAB 121 rescinded & GENIUS Act SHIFT

SAB 121 rescinded (Jan 2025): removed the accounting barrier that kept custodied crypto on banks’ balance sheets. GENIUS Act (Jul 2025): federal standards for stablecoin reserves enacted. Together they cleared the path for bank digital-asset custody. FACT

SEC Crypto / AI stance AI

2025 shift: new SEC leadership rescinded SAB 121, dropped crypto enforcement actions. Spot Bitcoin ETFs approved (Jan 2024); spot Ethereum ETFs (Jul 2024). SEC proposed AI conflicts-of-interest rule (2023) — finalisation status uncertain. FINRA AI/ML guidance on supervisory systems, algo trading, communications. DATA GAP

The BENJI precedent — a category opener: On Aug 12, 2026 the SEC Division of Investment Management issued a no-action letter allowing Franklin Templeton’s registered funds (mutual funds/ETFs) to hold FOBXX/BENJI tokenised money-market-fund shares for cash management and securities-lending collateral. The relief waives Rule 17f-2 physical-vault provisions, subject to 12 conditions (segregated wallets, daily reconciliation, board approval, 3 annual accountant verifications). This is the first formal SEC accommodation for tokenised-fund custody inside registered vehicles. FACT

06 · AI & Operations

AI is the new battleground — and the cost curves now have published numbers.

BNY’s Eliza, State Street’s Alpha and Citi’s Custody+ represent three different bets on how operations will be priced: agentic AI embedded across the franchise, a front-to-back data platform, and real-time single-event processing. AI differentiation will determine next-generation custody winners.

BNY Eliza AI

Enterprise AI platform (named after Eliza Hamilton); Eliza 2.0 launched Sep 2025; used by nearly all BNY employees daily. 125+ AI solutions in production (117 at Sep 30 2025, up 75% from Q2); 100+ digital employees deployed. −40% NAV false positives, −5% custody unit costs, 88% faster payment validation, 50% quicker contract processing; 99% of employees trained (20,000 building agents). Partners: Google Cloud (Gemini Enterprise), OpenAI, Microsoft, NVIDIA (first major bank to deploy AI Supercomputer). BNY oversees ~20% of world’s investable assets. FACT

State Street Alpha AI

First front-to-back investment platform; 5-year anniversary in 2024; open-architecture, cloud-native (Alpha Data Platform). 35 Alpha clients (25 live); 7 new mandates in 2024 (4 more in 2025). Alpha contributed ~50% of total AUC/A wins in 2024 and ~$380B of $1.1T new wins in Q2 2025. Front-office software & data revenue: $639M in 2024 (targeting $1B). $60T of assets on Charles River. Clients: Lazard, BMO AM, LGIM, Harris | Oakmark, Columbia Threadneedle, Challenger. FACT

Citi Custody+ AI

Launched Aug 18, 2026 — modular near- and real-time custody. Single Event Processing (SEP): patented; every asset-servicing transaction through one flow; US rollout complete. 80%+ of event volume in real-time; corporate-action processing time cut up to 92%. Digital-asset custody (Bitcoin) expected live “later this year” on common digital/traditional architecture. Citi Token Services live in US/UK/SG/HK; joined Swift 17-bank tokenised-deposit pilot (Jul 2026). >$2B/yr platform investment; AI-led tax-doc processing cut by up to 70%. FACT

1 · Front-to-Back Transformation — Alpha Platform Pitch AI

Buyer: Mid-to-large asset managers ($50B–$500B AUM) · Markets: US, Canada
Trigger
Legacy OMS/PMS replacement; desire to consolidate front, middle and back office onto one platform; accumulated technology debt.
Proposition
State Street Alpha delivers a unified front-to-back platform — Charles River (front office), Alpha Data Platform (middle), custody + fund admin + TA (back). Eliminates technology debt and enables real-time data.
Proof points
35 Alpha clients (25 live); Harris | Oakmark went live on Alpha (~$104B AUM); Columbia Threadneedle ($431B mandate); Alpha contributed ~$380B of Q2-2025 wins; front-office software revenue $639M (targeting $1B).
Landmine
BNY competes with integrated custody+TA+fund accounting (no separate front-office platform); JPMorgan may bundle with banking. Long implementation cycles (12–18 months). Client concern about “all eggs in one basket” with a single provider.

2 · AI-Driven Operational Alpha — Eliza / NAV Optimization AI

Buyer: Fund administrators, asset managers struggling with NAV errors & reconciliation breaks
Trigger
High NAV false-positive rates; manual reconciliation costs; T+1 compressed timelines; inability to hire ops staff at growth pace.
Proposition
BNY’s AI-powered NAV validation reduces false positives by 40% and custody unit costs by 5%. Eliza embeds AI across custody, payments and reconciliation with institutional-grade governance — sell “operations as an outcome.”
Proof points
125+ AI solutions in production; 99% employee adoption; 88% faster payment validation; $62.6T AUC/A data advantage; first bank with NVIDIA AI Supercomputer; 100+ digital employees.
Landmine

Under the SEC’s stance the client stays responsible — sell governance artefacts (model register, testing, audit evidence) as part of the service. Demonstrate specific, auditable outcomes.

11 · Risks & Honest Caveats

What would make this thesis wrong — say it before the client does.

Credibility with an MD audience comes from the caveats, not the hype. These are the honest counterweights to the growth narrative above.

The Safeguarding Rule is still pending

The SEC’s proposed Safeguarding Rule (Rule 223-1) remains not finalised as of Sep 2026. If adopted it expands custody scope to all client assets (crypto, real estate, derivatives) and imposes 12–18-month compliance windows. Until it lands, digital-asset custody demand is soft — and when it lands, it will trigger a wave of compliance-driven re-platforming. Sell readiness, not certainty. ASSESSMENT

Tokenised AUM is still a rounding error

~$39B of tokenised RWA against ~$60T+ of NA fund assets. The growth rate is the story, not the stock. BUIDL peaked ~$2.88B. Never present tokenisation as a current revenue pool; present it as an option-value and switching-event machine. Long-term forecasts span $2T (McKinsey) to $30T (Standard Chartered) by 2030–2034 — a 15x range that telegraphs real uncertainty. FACT

AI numbers are vendor-disclosed, not audited

The −40% / −5% figures come from BNY’s own disclosures; peers disclose less, making comparison hard. Quote them as “published by BNY,” never as industry benchmarks. The SEC’s proposed AI conflicts rule is not finalised; FINRA AI guidance citation needs verification. AI failures land on the client under current regulation — governance is a genuine cost, not a slide. DATA GAP

Old rails won’t die — they’ll coexist for years

Traditional TA systems will run alongside blockchain rails for years, with trillions staying on existing infrastructure. The realistic sale is hybrid servicing, which is harder operationally than either pure world — and that difficulty is precisely the service opportunity. Don’t oversell a “big bang” migration. ASSESSMENT

Scale could entrench the incumbents

8 consecutive quarters of collective AUC/A growth among the top 10, and consolidation is driving business to the largest custodians. A challenger or specialist without platform/AI/digital capability may find the window narrowing rather than opening — differentiation must be on a flank (private markets, real-time custody, tokenisation) the Big 4 do not yet cover natively. ASSESSMENT

Conflict risk in ETF servicing

State Street is both #1 ETF servicer and #3 ETF issuer (SPDRs). Issuers may avoid the servicer that is also a competing issuer. Active-ETF newcomers may prefer boutique servicers. This is a real competitive constraint on the largest ETF-servicing play. ASSESSMENT
