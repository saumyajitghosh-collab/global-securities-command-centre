01 · Market Sizing

A capital pool growing faster than the servicing capacity around it

Gulf asset management grew $2.2 trillion in AUM on a +9% trend, with Saudi Arabia and the UAE as the principal retail-mutual-fund growth engines. Layered on top sits $4.9 trillion of sovereign-wealth-fund assets — a historic peak — projected to climb toward $18 trillion by 2030. The servicing infrastructure is racing to catch up. FACT

GCC asset management FACT

GCC AUM reached $2.2 trillion in 2024, +9% YoY, against global AUM of $128 trillion (+12%); the Middle East & Africa region grew 10%. Saudi Arabia and the UAE are the principal growth drivers in retail mutual funds.

Sovereign-wealth-fund gravity FACT

Five largest Gulf SWFs collectively manage ~$4.349 trillion. Middle East SWF AUM hit a historic peak of $4.9T in 2024 (PwC), projected $7.3T, with Gulf SWFs forecast to control $18 trillion by 2030 — a ~50% increase (Deloitte/ORF). Globally, SWF assets crossed $15T in Dec 2025; combined SWFs + public pensions + central-bank assets = $60T.

Saudi REIT market FACT

Regulated under the REITs Instructions (CMA, October 2016). Closed-ended structure, min SAR 100M capital, ≥75% assets in developed income-generating real estate, ≥200 public unitholders, ≥50% public ownership, ≥90% net income distributed annually, borrowing capped at 50% of total asset value. REIT managers must appoint a CMA-licensed independent custodian + specialised property management company. Units register with Edaa (Depositary Centre) and trade on Tadawul.

DIFC & ADGM fund structures FACT

Both operate under English common law (DIFC/DFSA est. 2004; ADGM/FSRA est. 2013/2015). Three fund categories: Exempt Funds (professional, ~$50K min), Qualified Investor Funds (QIFs, ~$500K min), Public Funds (retail). ADGM offers the Protected Cell Company (PCC) structure — unavailable in DIFC — plus a dedicated VC Manager Regime (VCFM) with zero regulatory capital. Base capital: DIFC Exempt/QIF = $70,000; ADGM Exempt/QIF = $50,000.

Cross-border distribution: A funds passporting regime runs between mainland UAE, DIFC and ADGM. Since April 2024, foreign fund promotion in the UAE is limited to private offerings to professional investors — and retail funds must be domiciled in the UAE, prompting global managers to domicile in ADGM/DIFC. ADGM FSRA Consultation Paper No. 12 of 2025 proposes lighter regimes for Small Threshold Fund Managers (<$200M committed) and Institutional Fund Managers. FACT
04 · Regulatory Radar

Two simultaneous resets are tearing the buyer landscape open

Saudi Arabia is removing the QFI regime entirely (effective 1 Feb 2026), while the UAE reconstitutes its Securities & Commodities Authority as a Capital Market Authority (1 Jan 2026) — the most comprehensive overhaul of federal securities regulation since 2000. Together they redefine who can buy, who must be local, and where digital assets sit. FACT

1 FEB 2026 · SAUDI CMA
QFI regime removed entirely

From 6 Jan 2026, the Qualified Foreign Investor regime is abolished, effective 1 Feb 2026 — all foreign investors can invest directly in Tadawul Main Market shares. Capital Market Institutions Regulations amended (26 May 2025): custody/dealing/managing must be established in the Kingdom as joint stock companies or subsidiaries.

Implication: the local-presence moat widens even as market access opens. FACT
MAR 2026 · SAUDI CMA
Two new fund frameworks

Simplified Investment Funds (SIFs) and Financing Investment Funds (FIFs) introduced. Edaa introduced Omnibus Accounts for shares (Oct 2024 amendments).

New fund vehicles = new servicing demand. FACT
1 JAN 2026 · UAE
SCA → Capital Market Authority transformation

Under Federal Decree-Law No. 32/33 of 2025, the SCA is reconstituted as the CMA — the most comprehensive overhaul of UAE federal securities regulation since 2000. Virtual assets brought within the CMA's capital-markets perimeter; CMA jurisdiction extends to financial activities in the free zones (ADGM, DIFC) and extraterritorially. Administrative fines up to AED 200M; criminal penalties up to AED 250M and imprisonment. Existing SCA licensees must regularise by 1 Jan 2027.

Compliance window = sales window. FACT
10 JUN 2025 · ADGM FSRA
Digital-asset framework streamlined

ADGM FSRA implemented amendments to its digital-asset framework, consolidating its position as a leading digital-asset jurisdiction.

ADGM is the regional DLT anchor. FACT
2030 · VISION 2030
Saudi capital-markets deepening

PIF targeting $1T+ AUM (achieved $925B in 2024). Government targets AUM at 40% of GDP by 2030 (from ~23% in 1H25). The Financial Sector Development Program aims to deepen capital markets.

Structural mandate pipeline. FACT
Don't miss the deadline: Existing UAE SCA licensees must regularise under the new CMA regime by 1 January 2027 — a hard stop that defines the remediation and onboarding pipeline for every licensee in the free zones and onshore. FACT
05 · Tokenisation & DLT

Abu Dhabi is staking the regional claim on regulated digital-asset custody

The UAE Central Bank's digital dirham is underway with cross-border CBDC pilots; SAMA is exploring digital currency; and ADGM's FSRA framework has drawn both BNY and Standard Chartered into digital-asset custody inside the Emirate. The money layer and the asset layer are being built in parallel. FACT

UAE digital dirham DLT

UAE Central Bank digital dirham project underway, with cross-border CBDC pilots alongside India and other partners — the money-layer foundation for tokenised settlement in the region.

SAMA digital currency DLT

SAMA (Saudi Central Bank) exploring digital currency projects; PIF investing in tech infrastructure including blockchain. Saudi is building the asset side before the settlement side.

ADGM DLT framework DLT

ADGM FSRA's comprehensive digital-asset framework (amended 10 Jun 2025) is the regional anchor — recognised as a leading digital-asset jurisdiction, drawing BNY and Standard Chartered custody operations.

10 · Risks & Caveats

Where the thesis can break — and what to watch

The Middle East opportunity is real but concentrated and politically mediated. Four risks discipline every forecast in this module. ASSESSMENT

Regulatory transition FACT

UAE CMA requires existing licensees to regularise by 1 Jan 2027 — a hard stop that could disrupt service continuity for clients mid-migration.

Localisation requirements FACT

KSA mandates a local entity structure (joint stock company or subsidiary) for custody/dealing/managing — a cost and timeline barrier that narrows the field.

Geopolitical risk ASSESSMENT

Regional tensions could affect capital flows and the pace of foreign-investor participation — hard to forecast, material when it moves.

Concentration risk ASSESSMENT

The top five SWFs dominate the buyer landscape — a small number of relationships carry an outsized share of the mandate pipeline.

Honest caveat: SWF relationship economics, internal-vs-external management splits, and mandate-size ranges in this module are assessments, not disclosed figures. PIF's ~70% externally managed and ADIA's ~60% externally managed are estimates — verify against live RFPs before sizing a pipeline. The $18T-by-2030 SWF projection is a third-party forecast (Deloitte/ORF), not a commitment. DATA GAP
What we do not yet know: the precise servicing share BNY's Abu Dhabi digital-custody operation will capture; how the UAE CMA's extraterritorial reach into ADGM/DIFC will interact with the free zones' existing regulators; and the take-up rate of the new Saudi SIF/FIF fund vehicles after March 2026. These are live data gaps to monitor. DATA GAP
